# Play-test profiles (owner-approved plan, 12 Nov 2026)

A session is how long the player plays in one sitting, in GAME hours (1 game hour ≈ 17 real seconds at 1×, so 36 game hours ≈ 10 minutes).
Between sessions the app is closed: `away H` (real hours; production keeps filling the trays, level timers keep running, nothing attacks).

| Profile | Wants | Habit (session / gap) |
|---|---|---|
| New player | Only knows what the game tells them. Reads the cards and the radio, follows the mission, does not know any hidden numbers. Gets confused easily and says so. | 36 game h, then away 10 h; 36 game h, then away 14 h (two short sessions a day) |
| Builder | The biggest, best-run town. Hates idle land, idle people and waiting. Plans builds, upgrades, beds, land. | 140 game h, then away 22 h (one long evening a day) |
| Defender | Nobody dies. Best walls, guard posts, gear, traps. | 72 game h, then away 20 h |
| Raider | Fights factions; wants loot and gear; strongholds and raids. | 72 game h, then away 20 h |
| Explorer | Every destination, every stronghold, every lead. | 72 game h, then away 20 h |
| Collector | Every field note, trophy, medal and item. | 140 game h, then away 22 h |
| Caretaker | Bonds, pets, happy people; hates anyone getting hurt or sad. | 24 game h, then away 23 h (short, daily) |
| Rusher | Reach the City as fast as possible; skips anything that does not help. | 300 game h, then away 8 h (binge) |
