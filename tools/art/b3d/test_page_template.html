Survivor 3D Test

The Farmer, made from one ChatGPT drawing turned into a 3D model, rigged and rendered to sprites: all 13 game animations (plus hurt while armed) in four directions, with the bat, axe, rifle and AK-47. Melee weapons are one-handed and ride on the hand bone; guns ride on the chest with both hands locked to them. Swooshes, hit sparks and muzzle flash are rendered in 3D with the frames.

The same rigged model, animations and weapons as the sprites, drawn live in the browser with three.js: toon shading in hard bands and an ink outline. Drag (or one finger) to turn all the way round, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two fingers to pan. Game view snaps back to the game's 2:1 tile angle. The Equip, Animation and Speed controls above drive it too.

Two Farmers walk loops, turning at each corner so all four directions show; two more attack in place (swing for melee, fire for guns, punch empty-handed). The town is the ink mockup at the scale where its own villagers match the sprites. Equip changes every Farmer at once.
