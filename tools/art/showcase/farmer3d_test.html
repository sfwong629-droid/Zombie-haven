Zombie Haven · art-direction-b · test 1

Farmer, made in 3D

A low-poly Farmer built and animated in Blender, then rendered to flat sprite sheets with the game's fixed camera. The game stays 2D; only the images change. Built to judge the Last Day on Earth look and how smooth the movement feels.

In the yard

He walks a square in all four directions, stops at each corner, and throws a punch at one. Every direction is the same model turned, never a mirror.

What this test is

Made withBlender 5.2 (Python script), in the Claude workspace. No PixelLab credits used.
CameraOrthographic, 45° turn, 30° tilt, so ground tiles are still an exact 2:1.
Frame size256 × 288 rendered, shown at 128 × 144 (sharp on phones)
Framesidle 24 · walk 16 · punch 14, per direction
DirectionsSE, SW, NW, NE from one model. Adding all 8 costs only render time.
ModelRigid parts on a simple joint rig, colours taken from the approved Farmer. A rough stand-in for a proper Meshy/Tripo model with painted textures.

Not done yet

  • Painted textures and a softer, skinned body (elbows and knees are rigid joints here).
  • Real shadow under the feet; this page draws a soft blob.
  • Not wired into the game yet; buildings are still the pixel versions.
