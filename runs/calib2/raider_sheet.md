## What decided the fights

**Did change the outcome**
- **Squad power against the recommended 175, when it was far below.** F4 at 114 lost with the boss still at 100% (D4 08). The same two people at 178 won (F5, D5 08). That is the only loss in eight runs.
- **`sh med`.** One med gives +50 health at once, picks the lowest person, has no cooldown and works at second 0. F6 (D5 15): the pair started at 60%/30%, I pressed it 4 times, and Marcus finished at 100%. Without it the same pair, starting at 100%/100%, finished 33%/20% (F5). I ended the test with 30 meds (D6 14), so the supply was never a limit.
- **`sh focus` once the boss is awake, for speed.** F6: boss 88% → 2% in 10 s after the call (t45→t55). F5 with no call and one fighter left: 61% → dead in 38 s. F8: boss dead 14 s after waking. The price was visible: in F8 15 enemies were left alive and three of four people ended at 20%.
- **A working ranged weapon.** With the crossbow Marcus was at 100% through F6, F7 and F8 and until Lena went down in F5. With the empty shotgun he lost 27–52 points in F1–F3.

**Did not change the outcome**
- **Weapon quality on a four-person squad.** F3 with a kitchen knife (atk 1) and machetes won in 60 s; F1 with shotgun, rifle and 30 rounds won in 71 s. The cost was health only (F3 ended 20/45/20/11 against F1's 48/20/100/2).
- **Calls on a four-person squad.** F1 (no calls, 71 s) and F2 (three meds and focus, 65 s) both won. Calls only moved the health left afterwards.
- **Calling focus early.** In F8 I called it at t0; nothing happened until the boss woke at t35.
- **Risk taken, for loot.** Every win paid "renown 25, scrap 6" (9 once) plus two gear items, whether it was four people at power 320 (F8) or two at 178 (F5).

**Not proven either way**
- Whether starting health matters on its own. The power number did not move with it (178 at 100%/100% on D5 08 and 178 at 60%/30% on D5 15), yet in F4 Lena went from 60% to 9% in the first 10 s. F4 differed from F5 in gear, level and health at once, so I cannot separate them.
- Ammo. F1 had 30 rounds, F2 had none, and F2 also used calls and started more hurt.

**The pattern was the same every time.** The boss woke at 20–21 kills in seven of eight runs (t35–t45), then the fight ended 14–48 s later with 7–15 enemies still alive.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D5 08, F5 — two people at power 178 vs 175. Lena went down at t50 with the boss at 61%, and Marcus finished it alone over 38 s, dropping 100% → 33%. The only fight where I did not know the result in advance.
2. D4 15 — Bladesmithing finished, two Katanas (atk 32) crafted for 21 scrap each, and the pair's power went from 114 (D4 08) to 170. Preparation that showed in the number and then in the result.
3. D5 15, F6 — a Kevlar Vest (def 8, better than my Riot Vests at 7) came out of F5 and F6, and a second Crossbow out of F8 (D6 07). Gear worth equipping.

## Three moments that lowered it
1. D3 11, F3 — the squad armed with a kitchen knife won faster (60 s) than the gun squad (71 s, F1). The good gear in the stash felt pointless at four people.
2. D5 15, F6 — three meds in the first 10 s took a 60%/30% pair to 100%/100%. From then on I knew any fight could be bought with meds, and I had 16 left.
3. D1 08–10 — F1 spent all 30 rounds, the result did not say so, and the card explaining ammo arrived at D1 10:00, after the fight. I could not make or buy ammo for the rest of the test (ammo 0 until D5, 4 on D6 14), so both guns were clubs from F2 on.

## Waiting with nothing to do
- Healing. From D2 08 to D2 13 Marcus (75%), Lena (76%) and Ben (75%) did not move in 5 game hours on the Recover policy with a Medical Tent and two Medics. Same on D3 08–11 (Marcus 66%, Lena 75%, Ben 75%) and at D4 08, 20 hours after F3 (75/60/75). The only time hurt fighters reached 100% before day 6 was the wandering medic card on D5 08.
- There was nothing for a raider to do in those hours: the other fights on the map were locked (prison "locked", mall "Needs Haven level 6").

## Over too quickly or too easy
- Every fight ran 49–88 s. The four-person squad never lost (F1, F2, F3, F8), even with the knife.
- The Clinic was "cleared" on D1 09 and was back at full strength on D2 13, and twice within an hour on D6 (F7 at 06, F8 at 07). No cooldown and no rising difficulty: 30–35 enemies every time.
- A loss cost nothing but health: F4 gave "The squad was overrun and dragged itself home", both at 20%, nobody lost. The retreat in F7 cost nothing at all.

## Stuck
- D3 11 to D4 08: `research blades` refused with "needs 20 research points (have 20) and 5 scrap (have 87)" on three tries (D3 11, D3 12, D3 22). It went through at D4 08 with 29 points. Got out by waiting.
- D4 08: a fourth Rain Barrel was refused: "no spot in the build area will take it". Got out by expanding the land (D4 09).

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
Half and half. Served it: the stronghold was open from the first minute, power is one clear number, the two-person run near 175 was a real fight, and the gear drops were worth having. Got in the way: the recommended power is set so low that four level-6 people are already above it on paper with nothing equipped (196, D1 08; I did not fight ungeared, but the knife squad at 235 won, F3), the calls are stronger than any preparation, loot does not grow with risk, and the 75% health plateau left long stretches with nothing to do.

## The one change you would make
Put a limit on `sh med` during a stronghold fight (a cooldown, or two or three uses per fight) and raise the Clinic's recommended power so that four level-6 people are not above it before they pick up any gear (196 ungeared, D1 08; the knife squad at 235 won, F3). Then gear, squad choice and the timing of the calls would decide the fight, as they did only in F5.
