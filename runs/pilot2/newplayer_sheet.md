# Evaluation sheet — New player · build 3D.161 · seed 21 · days 1–20

Played D1 08:00 to D20 00:00 in 13 sessions (36 game hours each, `away 10` / `away 14` alternating; three sessions ended 1–2 h early or late because a card stopped play). Evidence: `runs/pilot2/newplayer.jsonl` and `runs/pilot2/newplayer_notes.md`.

End state D20 00: Haven level 5 (reached D7 10, stuck there 13 days), renown 419, 7 people on the list, **14 deaths**, food 52, water 38, scrap 69, wood 6.

## Fun score: 4 / 10
D1–D6 were a pleasant 7 (things got built, people arrived, a child was rescued). D7–D15 were a 2: I watched 13 people die over eight days with no button to press, and I could not find out how to reach level 6.

1. D6 08 → D6 15 — "A lost child" card, sent Marcus & Amy, got "Ivy is safe and will grow up in the Haven. +8 Renown". At D9 00 "IVY HAS GROWN UP" plus "LEGACY: NEXT GENERATION … +15 Renown · +10 scrap". On D15 08 Ivy was the last one standing (100%, the other 7 at 0%) and carried Ezra to the Medical Tent, who then carried Marcus (D15 15). That is the story I will remember.
2. D8 08 and D11 08 — Greenwater Commune "Trade 6 food for 10 water". Water was 8.9 and 1.8 at those moments. The first clear, fair lever on my worst problem.
3. D16 08 — "A CONVOY FOUND THE HAVEN … Felix (Farmer) and Cass (Scavenger) climb down to stay, and they unload 15 food, 15 water and some scrap." I had 3 people left. Food 31.9→47.9, water 13.4→30.8. Then D16 19 "Maya bought a Riot Vest at the Armory (−7 scrap)" and D17 morning "Walkers stopped: 16 … Hurt: Ivy, Cass" — it finally felt like a town that could defend itself.

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
My goal was just to follow what the game told me. For five days it served that well: the radio requests (second garden D1 19, scrapyard D2 12, lodge D3 02, armory D5 11) gave me a next step each. After that it got in the way:
- It told me things I could not do: "upgrade the Camp to a Lodge … Tap your Camp, then Upgrade" at D3 02, but "not unlocked yet" until level 4 (D4 10).
- It never told me the things that killed the town: that I needed more than one Guard (first hint was "Defense: 0/20" on the D8 00 review, after the first death), that a toll was coming and how much scrap to keep, that medical beds were a limit ("no medical bed free: slower care" first seen D11 23).
- It kept inviting people in ("I'd open the gate") while water was under 3 days.

## The one change you would make
When people start going down, give the player something to do and tell them why it is happening: a clear "you have 1 Guard for 9 people — train more" warning before the first boss night (D6), and a tappable action on a downed survivor (carry now / use meds), instead of 19 hours of "is rescuing". Fourteen deaths that I could only watch is what took this from a 7 to a 4. (Second choice, cheap: say in the nextLevel line how to get a Banner.)
