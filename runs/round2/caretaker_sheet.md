# Evaluation sheet — Caretaker · build 3D.169 · seed 21 · days 1–20

Every claim cites a day and a number from caretaker_notes.md or caretaker.jsonl.

## Fun score: 6 / 10
The relationship side is the best thing in the game for this profile: couples, three babies, a child growing up, residents settling in, feasts. But from D11 on, about every third session went entirely on rescue orders while 6–9 of 12 people lay bleeding. Nobody died, but it often felt tense and helpless, not cozy.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D5 08 — The feast card (6 food, 6 water) gave "+15 ♥ for everyone · bonds grow". Four friendships formed on the spot, and Marcus went from ♥31 to ♥50. Over D5 07–13, Ben, Marcus, Amy and Rosa asked to become residents and lost their "no House of their own" complaint. That was the first time the town felt like mine.
2. D10 00 → D15 00 → D16 00 → D20 00 — Ben+Eli became a couple and had baby Ivy the same night (D10 00). Ivy grew up five days later (D15 00, "18 yrs · Loner"). Finn was born to Marcus+Amy (D15 00), and Nora and Kai were born on D16 00. Finn grew up on D20 00. Having a "Next generation" legacy goal for it is a great fit for this profile.
3. D17 14 — Theo, the one person who stayed lonely (♥3 on D5, ♥15 on D8, ♥18 on D12, always "no House of their own, no friends yet"), finally asked to stay for good after 12 days. Accepting him felt earned. The Armory also stood out (D9 09 onward): people buy their own crowbars, riot vests and Hawaiian shirts. It is charming and protective.

## Three moments that lowered it
1. D18 00–18 — In my worst session, 9 of 12 adults were down at D18 08. Only Marcus (38%), Amy, Ben (10%) and Noah were standing, and 10–11 walkers stayed in town in daylight. Radio help was "not ready" because I had just paid the favour, and `militia` said "healthy civilians band together when no fighter is left". Getting people to care took 16 h for Lena (down D18 00, Clinic D18 16), 15 h for Eli, and 14 h for Maya (down D18 04, reached the Lodge with 5 h left). I spent the whole 24 h session ordering rescues.
2. D11 06 and D16 06 — The morning cards read "Hurt: Amy, Ben, June, Theo, Eli, Maya, Lena, Wes" (8 of 11) and "Hurt: Amy, Rosa, June, Noah, Wes, Lena, Eli, Ivy" (8 of 12). Medical buildings got wrecked with patients inside: Clinic D11 06 (spitter), Medical Tent D8 00, Clinic D16 09 (bloated, Amy inside), Clinic D18 07, Clinic D19 17. For a caretaker, watching the hospital fall with the wounded in it every few nights is the worst thing the game does.
3. D1–D14, low-risk trips — "Lena was hurt" on D1 at Abandoned Houses (Low). "Eli was hurt" on D2 and D4. "Lena collapsed and is being carried" on D4 09 and again on D14 10 at Lumber Yard (Low), with no card or choice beforehand. On D11 13, a sneak that succeeded at 87% still took Lena from 75 to 27 HP. Picking every "safe" button did not keep people safe, so the choices felt like they didn't matter.

## Waiting with nothing to do

- Level timers were long (L4 48 h, L5 60 h, L6 3 days, L7 84 h, L8 92 h, L9 92 h), but they mostly finished during the 23 h away (L4, L5, L6, L9). - Wood was a constant choke. On most mornings it sat at 2–8 (D4 06: 3, D6 07: 5, D8 06: 7, D14 07: 5, D18 08: 2). I was always waiting on a lumber trip to build anything.

- Bosses (D6, D9, D12, D15, D18) all died or left by morning. The damage came from the ordinary walkers and raiders around them.

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
Both, sharply.
- **It served the goal:** friendships, couples, babies, residents and feasts are all there, and they show up through the mood lines. By D15, 10 of 12 people were ♥72–102 "happy".
- **It got in the way:** keeping everyone unhurt was impossible. Almost every night 3–8 people were downed. The game's own scoring disagrees with what I saw: Week 1 Happiness 6/20 (D8 00) and Week 2 Happiness 3/20 (D15 00), while nearly everyone sat at ♥100 "happy". That made me unsure what "happy" means.
- **Pets:** pets were what this profile most wanted, and through this interface they did nothing. Scout (D3 00) and Rex (D6 00) "found the Kennel. Tap a survivor → Pet". I also bought a cat (D9 13, "sold": true). None of them ever appeared in state or the stash.

## The one change you would make
Make night damage to the town's people hit fewer of them at once, and make care reliable: zombies should not break into Medical Tents and Clinics that have patients inside, and a "rescue" order should reach the medical bed within an hour or two, not 14–16 h (D18). One bad night that downs two or three people is drama. Downing 6–9 of 12 every few nights (D11, D16, D18, D19) turns a cozy bond-building game into a rescue-order chore for anyone who cares about the people.
