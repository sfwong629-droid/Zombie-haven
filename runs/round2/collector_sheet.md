# Evaluation sheet — Collector · build 3D.169 · seed 21 · days 1–17 (town lost D17 13:00)

Every claim cites a day and a number from my notes (collector_notes.md) or the run log (collector.jsonl).

## Fun score: 4 / 10
For about 14 days the game gave a collector plenty to collect: 16 zombie field notes, 9 weapon recipes (D2), then 17 armour recipes (D10) and 14 accessory recipes (D15), plus legacy goals (4/8 by D14). Then one boss night (D15 21:00, Stalker with 15 zombies) put all 14 people down by D16 18:00, and every one of them had died by D17 13:00. I could not do anything to stop it. On top of that, a lot of the collection could not be used or claimed through the tool (dogs, goals, field-notes screen). Without the wipe I would have scored this 6.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D10 08–12 — First Shopping Mall trip (medium risk, power 163): +57 food, +32 scrap, +32 cloth, Lifting Gloves and a Back Brace, and both event rolls (53% and 82%) went my way. One trip was worth about 8 lumber runs. Finding loot like this is what this profile plays for.
2. D9 00:00 and D14 00:00 — the LEGACY cards ("A Real Town", "Far Horizons", "Next Generation", "Know-How": 4/8, +15 renown and +10 scrap each) and "Complete 4 and reach ★5 to call for the convoy". A numbered list of goals to finish is exactly what a collector wants.
3. D7 05:00 and D10 05:00 — beating the Butcher dropped a Shotgun ("🎁 Found: Shotgun"). Beating the Bloater Queen dropped a Riot Vest (def 7). Boss trophies felt like real rewards. The Greenwater thread was a smaller high: I sent 2 meds on D6, and on D8 "The child is well" paid back +12 food and +2 cloth.

## Three moments that lowered it
1. D15 21:00 → D17 13:00 — the town was wiped. The Stalker came with 15 zombies. By D16 03:00 the log said "12 down, too few left to fight". Walkers stayed inside the town in daylight (12 at D16 08:00, 10 at D16 18:00), and the morning card counted 561 damage. Every lever failed. `radiohelp` said "not ready" because I had paid the earlier favour at D16 00:00. `militia` was refused ("only when no fighter is left") while every fighter was down. Quarantine Command said "We're sending a team" at D16 07:00, and no team came. `bandage` used up the last 16 cloth. Then 14 people died between D17 02:00 and D17 13:00. Only 42 h earlier the Week 2 review had graded me S, with "Defense 20/20".
2. D7 09:00 — June died on a LOW-risk Lumber Yard run, for +8 wood. The horde card showed her at 80/80 HP and I pressed "Fight through". The report says "June did not make it" at stage 2, then "no orders came, so Theo chose Turn back now". It felt arbitrary, and the report contradicted what I had done.
3. D3 08:00 / D4 00:00 / D10 08:00 — I adopted 2 stray dogs (Rex, then a second "Pepper") and got a third dog from the Kennel ("Pepper", D4). Each card said "Give the dog to a survivor from their Gear tab" or "Tap a survivor → Pet". None of the dogs ever showed up in the stash, and `equip Marcus rex/dog/pepper/pet` always failed with "not in the stash". For a collector, three pets I own and can never use is a sore point. (This may partly be a tool gap. See the test-tool section.)

- The trader was announced on D5 09:00 and D12 09:00 and never came either time.

## Over too quickly or too easy
- Couples and babies happen instantly. Rosa and Theo became a couple and baby Ivy was born in the same hour (D8 00:00). Nora was born on D17 00:00 while every adult was lying on the ground bleeding out.
- Medium-risk trips pay far more than low-risk ones for similar danger. Lumber Yard medium gave +43 wood (D13) and +39 wood (D14), against 8–16 from low. On low runs people were still hurt or killed (D2 Amy collapsed on a solo low Pharmacy run; D7 June died).
- The Week reviews seem wrong. Week 1 gave Happiness 3/20 while hearts were 29–75. Week 2 gave Defense 20/20 the night before a total wipe, and Happiness 5/20 with hearts of 33–100.

## Stuck
- Workshop: `upgrade workshop` said "no room around the old one (clear a neighbouring tile)" on D6 and again on D14. No command clears a tile, but on D14 the Clinic upgrade "placed the bigger building over the old one". I never got the Workshop.
- Build area full on D11 18:00 ("no spot ... 29 occupied, 21 entrance blocked, 15 wall"). Expanding land needs HQ tier 3 (20 wood, 30 stone, 12 scrap, 2 fuel). I never had 30 stone (I had 7 from D7 to the end), so the town could not grow past 16 beds.
- Walls never sealed. On D6 13:00, D6 and D14 10:00, `walls` placed 4, then 1, then 6 tiles, and every time it said "sealed:false, gap 15,15: an open tile". The same gap stayed open for 8 days.
- The final wipe (D16). I tried rescue, bandage, radiohelp, militia, defend and policy recover. None of it got me out.

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
Mostly it served the goal until D15. The amount of collectable content is the best thing about this build for this profile: 16 field notes, 40 crafting recipes, 4 bosses each with their own note, trader stock that kept changing (Goalie Pads, Tactical Cap, Chef item, SMG, cat, grenades) and legacy goals. Three things got in the way:
- Loot is thrown away at the cap without a warning. On D11 the mall brought +47 cloth, but cloth stayed at 20 (the cap). Food was clamped at 80.
- Goals said "Claim the reward from the alert or the Goals screen" about 10 times (D1, D2 ×2, D5, D7, D8 ×2, D14 ×2, D15). Legacy 4/8 said "the radio tower can call for the convoy (Goals)". I never got to claim or act on any of these.
- The pets never worked (see Low 3).

Finally, the wipe erased the whole collection (all wearers died) after 17 days of collecting.

## Problems with the test tool (not the game)
- There is no `goals` / claim command, so I could never claim about 10 finished goals or the convoy (Legacy 4/8).
- There is no pet command. Pets are not in the stash, so I could never give a dog to anyone.
- There is no command to place or rotate a 2×1 building ("The Workshop is 2×1: place it (turn it with ↻)") or to clear a tile.
- There is no car-alarm command (the radio tip on D3 00:00: "tap a wreck near them").
- `cart` is in help with no description. I typed it on D5 20:00 and it built a Hand Cart without showing the cost. Wood went from 14 to 0 by D6 06:00.
- `upgrade hq2` on D5 11:00 returned `ok:false` with an empty reason (the real cause was the two-build limit). `levelup` while already upgrading also returns `ok:false` with no reason.
- Explosives bought from the trader (pipe bombs on D9, grenades on D15) never appear in `stash`, so I could not see how many I had.
- `away 22` credited only 8 game hours both times (D7, D14). That may be by design, but the tool does not say so before you go away.
- Session habit: I overran my second 140 h session. I went away at D14 11:00 instead of about D13 03:00, roughly 32 game hours late.

## The one change you would make
Make sure a strong town can survive one bad boss night. When the last fighter goes down, make sure there is always a way to recover: radio help that is not locked out by a favour paid the hour before, militia allowed when every fighter is down, and walkers leaving at dawn instead of staying inside all day. A collector can live with losing a fight. Losing 17 days of collection (15 deaths) in 36 game hours, with nothing left to press, is what made me want to quit.
