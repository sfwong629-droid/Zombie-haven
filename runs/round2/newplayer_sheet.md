## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
My goal was a cozy, growing town where nobody dies. Up to D10 the game served it well: Dusk Radio told me what to build (barrel D1, second garden D3, Lodge D3 19), the cards were readable, and the friendships and couples were charming.

## The one change you would make
Don't let a town lose everyone from nights alone with no lever left to pull. When most of the town is down, give the player a guaranteed recovery option that costs something but never needs scrap or meds the town no longer has. A free basic bandage from the HQ, or a rescue team that is always available for a renown price, would both work. Also say plainly in the day report when the fence is open or the policy is cutting production. Four of my five deaths (D19) came from running out of scrap, and that only happened because nightly repairs and bandages drained it without warning.
