## Fun score: 5 / 10
The bonds are lovely to read about (57 "now friends" lines, a couple on D9) and nobody died in 14 days. But I could never see how anyone felt. The first mood number I saw was "Happiness 4/20" on D8, after I'd done everything a caretaker does. And the same people collapsed night after night (30 collapses, June 8 of them) with nothing I could change to stop it.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D9 00:00: "A NEW COUPLE: Amy and Lena have grown close and are now a couple. They may start a family…". The bond had a visible history: Amy bandaged Lena on D2, they became "friends" D3 07:00 and "close friends" D7 01:00 right after Amy got Lena back on her feet. It was the best moment of the run.
2. D2 09:00 and D3 13:00: on the sick-stranger cards I picked "Treat him" (23% and 35%) and both worked: "Marcus brought the fever down. He gave them Crowbar" (+3 renown). D8 08:00: Greenwater's "A CHILD IS SICK", I sent 2 medicine and got "Greenwater will not forget". Choosing kindness paid off and read well.
3. D9 08:00: the "A FEAST? Spirits are low" card. I held it for 6 food and 6 water and got "+15 ♥ for everyone · bonds grow". Within the hour 9 new friendships appeared (D9 08:00–10:00). That was the one time caring made a visible difference I could see as a number.

Before this I had run Bonfire Night (D3 15), built a Lounge (D5 02), run a Harvest Feast (D7 18, 6 h before the review), welcomed all 12 askers, and lost nobody. No mood number appeared anywhere in state in 7 days. The only ♥ values I ever saw were on move-in cards (♥35–♥44), so I had no idea what to fix.

The tool has no pet command (`pet` = "unknown command"), and on D13 14 I bought a dog from the trader: "ok", then no name, no news and nothing in state. For a pet-loving profile, pets were a dead end.

Half and half.
- **What served the caretaker:** the friendship and couple lines, named fire-fighters ("thanks to Noah, Maya, Rosa", D5 01), rescues that turned into bonds ("Marcus carried Dante → now friends", D10 03; "Theo carried Lena → friends", D12 01), and the sick-person cards with a clear cheap Treat option ("Lena is sick … Treat (2 scrap, 3 water) → Lena will be fine", D4 08).   - There is no mood readout, so I couldn't see how people felt.
  - I couldn't keep the wounded out of fights.
