## Fun score: 5 / 10
Trips were the best part of the game. But I saw only 5 normal destinations in 15 days. Their event cards were the same 4 again and again. Medium risk downed people, and high risk killed two of them. The two raids I tried had waves that never ended. The town was busy keeping itself alive, which slowed my exploring.

## Three moments that raised it
1. **D5 09, Clinic stronghold.** I started with power 115 against a recommended 175. I watched the fight in slices and called `sh med` about 7 times. Lena went 23% → 73%, then June 47% → 98%. We killed the boss at 71 s with 23 kills, and the Haven got a banner. My calls mattered, and the fight felt tense.
2. **D2 09, first trip decision card ("Wrecked Armored Van").** It offered three options, each with a named person and a percentage (AGI Lena 72% / STR Marcus 53% / Leave). Lena climbed in and came out with Hockey Pads. Choosing the person by their stat felt good.
3. **D6 06 and D7 10, medium Lumber Yard trips.** These brought 43 and 48 wood, and no one died. Medium felt clearly better than low (14 wood on D2), and that is how risk should step up.

## Three moments that lowered it
1. **D10 07, high-risk Old Quarry.** Food fell 11 → 1.2 and water 10.5 → 3.2 within an hour of the squad leaving. The order never showed that cost. The low supplies then caused "Short on supplies: the trip is riskier", and "Eli did not make it". June also vanished: the report was cut off at stage 2, and I only knew she was dead because the people list went from 9 to 7. Two of my three best fighters died for 107 stone, which I never used.
2. **The same event cards over and over.** The Wrecked Van appeared at Houses on D2, D2, D4 and D6. The Barricaded Store appeared at the Pharmacy (D3), the Houses (D4, D5, D7, D8), the Lumber Yard (D7, D11) and the Quarry (D8). Cry for Help, Horde on the Road and Sick Stranger also came up at every destination. A Lumber Yard and a Pharmacy should not both have a "corner shop". Places differed only in what the loot line said.
3. **D9 10, Greenwater raid, and D15 15, Toll Kings raid.** Both started with about 30-36 s of nothing (0 kills). Then enemiesLeft kept refilling: 13 → 17 → 19 on D9, and 1 → 6 → 4 on D15, even after the boss was dead. On D9 I lost while above the recommended power (229 against 200). Greenwater then said "they will hit back", but on D10 08 they were back offering a trade. The D15 raid ended with the server crashing.

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
**Partly.** The parts I wanted are there: per-person stat checks, decisions with a timer, stronghold slices with med and focus calls, and leads that expire. But four things got in the way:
- **Low variety.** The shared event pool makes trips feel the same.
- **Risk does not scale.** Low risk downed people (D1, D5 ×2). Medium downed whole squads (D3: 2 of 2; D8: Lena). High killed 2 of 3 (D10).
- **Unclear unlocks.** The game never says that level 4 unlocks the Lodge. The faction raids I never met were not reachable in 15 days.
- **Missing reports.** I got none for the Mall (D13) or the D12 Pharmacy trip. Other reports were cut off mid-sentence (D1, D10).

Fuel and stone (30 and 140) had no use by D15, so two of the five destinations fed nothing. The town also kept pulling the squad home: on D12–D13 7 of 11 people collapsed in one night, and my fighters were still hurt from trips.

## The one change you would make
Give each destination its own event cards, and make the expedition order show the full cost and real odds before you send people. A Lumber Yard trip should feel like a lumber yard. A high-risk trip should say "packs 10 food, 7 water; about X% chance someone dies", so a death is a risk you chose, not a surprise in a truncated report.
