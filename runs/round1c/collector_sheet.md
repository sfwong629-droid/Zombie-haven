## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
For the first week it served it well. There was a steady drip of NEW cards, radio explanations, items from trips and clear unlock text such as "LOCKED: Needs the Lodge" and "Needs Haven level 7". D13 00h's "The next level needs a Banner … clear the Overrun Clinic … or finish a Trading Post" is exactly the "how to get it" signposting I want. From D8 on the game got in the way. Rewards arrived silently or not at all, I lost a scavenger with no explanation, duplicates piled up with no use, and the Armory NPCs overrode my gear: on D14 13h Maya swapped the Crossbow I gave her (atk 34) for a Scrap Spear. The level clock stretched past whole sessions, which hid most of the content (Military, Warehouse and Prison need level 7/9; seasonal strongholds need 15).

## Problems with the test tool (separate from the game)
- There is no command for Field Notes, trophies, medals or Goals, so I could not see or claim the 9+ "done! Claim the reward" goals, and I could not judge medals or trophies at all.
- There is no pet command. Dogs and the cat could not be assigned.
- Building upgrades that need placement (the 2×1 Workshop and Clinic) cannot be placed through `upgrade`.
- Long card text in `cardsRead` is cut off mid-word ("Stage 2: L…", D1 17h; "stripped t…", D8 17h).
- After an emoji card, the same card is printed again with the emojis stripped ("BANDIT", "Move-in request … 35"). This is cosmetic.
- `state full` printed some sections twice (D3 17h output).
- It is possible that the missing report and morning cards (D8+) are lost by the tool rather than the game; the run log should be checked.

## The one change you would make
Always give a result card for every trip, stronghold and night (morning). It should list each item found, mark it **NEW** or **duplicate (n owned)**, and say plainly when someone is down, missing or dead. Add a way to turn duplicates into something useful (sell or scrap them).
