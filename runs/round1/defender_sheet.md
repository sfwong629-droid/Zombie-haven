## Three moments that raised it
1. D14 06–08: radiohelp became ready with Eli 4 h from bleeding out. I bandaged 3 people (cloth 7 → 4) and the rescue team "carried 3 of your people into care" two hours later. The 7 cloth from the D13 houses trip saved them, so here my preparation paid off.
2. D12 12 and D15 12: the radio gave a 9-hour boss warning ("a boss comes with the horde at 21:00"). Both boss nights (Stalker D12, Bloater Queen D15) caused no new downs. D13 morning: 6 stopped, Hurt Marcus only.
3. D17 08: a convoy brought Omar and Sol plus 15 food, 15 water and 6 scrap when I had 4 people and 2 scrap. D17 14: radiohelp carried Eli and Sol in within 2 h. When it works, the radio is the best thing in the game.

## Three moments that lowered it
1. D15 06 → D16 17: four people were down at dawn. Marcus, Hana and Eli sat in "rescueTo" for 26+ h in daylight. I re-ordered rescue every 1–2 h, switched policy (produce/defend/recover), tried defend, and was refused militia. Nobody was ever picked up, and Maya, Noah, Amy and Ivy died one after another (D16 10, 12, 14, 17).
2. D17 08 and D9 08: the Toll Kings demanded 14 scrap when I had 2 (D17) or 8 (D9). Refusing was the only real choice, and each raid downed 3+ people (D9: 3 later died; D17: Eli, Omar, Sol). D19 08: the "Toll Collector" brought 10 more raiders, and by D20 00 everyone was down.
3. D18 21: a boss night (Stalker + 13) with no noon warning. The run log has "boss comes" lines on D12 12 and D15 12 but none on D18. The earlier tester saw the same on D9. With 4 already down, Hana (the medic) collapsed at 21:00.

- D15 06 → D16 17 and D18 06 → D19 04: about 50 game hours of re-ordering rescue and watching bleed-out timers count down. My only real lever was rationing bandages.

## Over too quickly or too easy
Nothing was too easy. Boss nights were milder than ordinary nights: D12 boss 0 downs, D15 boss 0 new downs, but the ordinary night of D15 01–05 put 4 down. I can't tell why.

## Stuck
- **Rescue never arrives while anyone is fighting.** Rescuers sit in "rescueTo" for 9–28 h (D13 15→D14 06, D15 06→D16 17, D18 06→D18 21). The only times people got picked up: (a) radiohelp (D14 08, D17 16); (b) after the message "N down, too few left to fight. They hide until the zombies move on, then go for the fallen" (D18 21–23 Marcus carried Omar and Sol; D19 07–08 Pia carried Omar and Sol). So rescue seems to lose out to combat or chasing. Nothing I tried (rescue re-orders, all 3 policies, `defend`, changing Marcus's job) got me out. I never got out of this loop.
- **Raiders linger for days.** D9 17 → D12 ~13 and D17 08 → D20 01, with "raiders" never reaching 0 for long. While they're on the map everyone "chases" and nothing heals.
- **Scrap.** Upkeep 2.4/day against scrap of 0–15 the whole time. The 10-scrap "walls" research (30 rp) stayed unaffordable from D11 to D20 even with 56+ rp banked (D16 18: rp 56, scrap 3).

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
It got in the way. My goal was "nobody dies, prepare for everything."
- **Warned in time?** Boss nights yes on D12 and D15 (9 h). D18 and D9 had no warning. Raiders came with only a few hours' notice ("7 raiders are sneaking", D12 11, D14 17, D18 11), and the Toll King tolls come with no warning at all.
- **Is preparation rewarded?** Partly. Cloth from trips (D13 +7) and paying the favour (D16 00, D19 00) kept the radio available, and that saved 5 people. Walls, gear and guards did not visibly matter. 98 wall tiles, still `sealed:false` (D14 08), and no tile count to go on. D18 morning: "Walkers stopped: 1" with 4 down.
- **Can I tell what my defences are worth?** No. The week 1 review said Defense 7/20 (D8 00) and week 2 said **Defense 0/20** (D15 00) after I added 8 wall tiles, a riot helmet and 2 guards. No number before a fight tells me whether I'll hold. `power` gives 194 for the whole town (D11 08) but means nothing for night defence.
- **Did any death feel like my mistake?** Mostly no. D9's trip and Ivy as an L1 guard were mine. But every death came after 16–36 h of "X is coming" with no arrival, and the bandage I needed most was refused at scrap 2 (D16 06–09).
- **Emergency orders.** `bandage` works (+5 h, 1 cloth or ~2–2.5 scrap) but is refused below 3 scrap. `rescue` returns ok but changes nothing. `militia` was always refused ("healthy civilians band together when no fighter is left"). `radiohelp` is the only order that saves anyone, and it recharges for days.

## The one change you would make
Make rescue beat combat. A downed person with a rescue order should be picked up within an hour or two, even while raiders or walkers are on the map. Failing that, say plainly *why* nobody is coming ("blocked by 3 raiders at the gate") and what would fix it. Six of my nine deaths (D16, D19) came from this alone. Every other system, from walls and gear to bandages and the radio, only bought time against it.
