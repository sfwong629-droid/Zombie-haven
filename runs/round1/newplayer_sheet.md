## Fun score: 3 / 10
Days 1–5 were fun and easy to follow. From about D9 the game turned into hour after hour of bandaging people I could not save. I never learned what I was doing wrong, so the deaths felt random and unfair rather than earned.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D4 and D5: radiohelp "THE RESCUE TEAM ARRIVED ... cleared 6 zombies ... carried 1 of your people into care." It came again on D8 05, and on D17 00 the game offered it on a card itself ("5 of your people are down"); that time it "cleared 14 zombies ... carried 5". It was the only thing that ever reliably turned a bad night around, and the biggest relief in the run.
2. D5 21: "The next level needs a Banner ... clear the Overrun Clinic, or finish a Trading Post or a Radio Tower." This was a clear goal with choices. I built the Trading Post on D7 14 and got "Civic Banner raised". This was the best teaching in the game.
3. D6 morning card: "Walkers stopped: 13 · Buildings damaged: 3 (76) · Hurt: nobody", after crafting a machete and a nail bat. It was the one time my preparation visibly paid off. D19 was a second, smaller case: "Walkers stopped 3, hurt nobody", and the Butcher boss night (D19 21) ended with nobody down.

## Three moments that lowered it
1. D17 21 to D18 04: seven of my people died in 7 game hours (Pia, Theo, Leo, Noah V., Amy, Rosa, Lena). The rescue team had carried 5 of them into care at D17 01, yet by D17 07 seven of 8 were down again with 11 walkers in town in daylight. Every `bandage` was refused ("needs 1 cloth (or scrap)", scrap 0 to 2), `militia` was refused ("when no fighter is left", but all my fighters were down) and radiohelp was "not ready (favour owed)". I had no move left.
2. D12 09 to D12 18: nothing moved for 9 game hours. Amy, Rosa and Pia were stuck on "rescueTo", and Marcus, Noah, Theo, Lena and Leo were stuck on "chase", against 6 walkers and 4–6 raiders that were never cleared. Maya, Hana and Eli bled out over the next day (D12 21, D13 03, D13 06). Only at D13 09 did the news explain it: "3 down, too few left to fight. They hide until the zombies move on."
3. D14 05: Marcus died (Guard L6, machete, my strongest person since day 1). He had been down since D13 12 (17 h), "NO rescuer", while I ordered `rescue Marcus` twice. That same night the boss (Bloater Queen, D13 21) arrived with no radio warning, although the D6, D16 and D19 bosses were all announced.

## Waiting with nothing to do
- Almost every session after D10 was spent waiting on rescues that did not happen. Examples: "Amy is rescuing Ben" on every news line from D10 10 to D11 20 (34 game h, Amy at 96–100% hp); Rosa down from D14 21 to D16 05 with "Amy is coming" (32 game h); Maya on "rescueTo" Pia from D17 08 to D17 20.
- The news feed was mostly rescue spam. At D12 08 one 1-hour `play` returned about 40 lines of "X is rescuing Y" (by D12 08 I filtered them out to read anything else).
- Level 6 upgrade: ordered at D11 09 with minutesLeft 180. It was still 171 at D11 20 (11 game hours later) and only finished during `away 10`. The "about N min while you play" counts real minutes, not game time, and nothing says so.
- From D16 to D20, wood piled up to 21–30 with nothing to spend it on, because every build also needs scrap ("mat").

## Over too quickly or too easy
- Levels 1 to 3 in two days (D2 18 level 2, then level 3 right after `away 10` on D2 21). It felt unearned. Then on D4 09 the radio said "Zombies grow tougher every day, but only up to a ceiling set by your Haven level", which I only learned after levelling three times.
- Water was never a real problem after D3. At D20 it is 95/95, and the D8 review gave "Supplies 20/20" while people died.

## Stuck
- **Scrap.** It was the real bottleneck, and I did not learn that until D10 18 ("Upkeep at midnight is 7.3 scrap and you have 0"). I did not learn where scrap comes from until D14 13 (Gas Station trip: +6 scrap). Houses and Lumber Yard trips gave none (D10 houses: food, water and cloth only). The trader on D10 17 and D15 15 sold only for scrap. The Toll Kings asked 18 scrap on D12 08. I tried: houses trips (cloth only), Salvage Methods research (D16 14, no visible gain), and gas station trips (worked, but medium risk and Maya collapsed on D16). I never really got out: scrap was 0–2 at D17 20 and 2 at D20 00.
- **Bandage refused while showing scrap.** I could not bandage at scrap 3 (D11 20), 2 (D12 00, D13 08, D17 12) or 1 (D12 18). After Field Medicine (D14 13) a scrap bandage worked on D15 01 (scrap 12). As a new player, "(or scrap)" read like a promise the game did not keep. People died on hours when I was trying to bandage them.
- **Rescue.** `rescue NAME` usually showed no change ("NO rescuer" stayed). The exceptions were D13 15 (Theo carried in 1 h after the order) and D16 09 in a calm morning. I never found out what makes it work.
- **Lumber Camp, Workbench, Canteen and Scrapyard** showed as "Open staff slot" in every day summary from D12 00 to D20 00. The game never told me which profession runs a Lumber Camp.

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
My goal was to follow what the game teaches. For days 1–5 it served that well: the radio walked me through barrel and garden, the banner goal was clear, and the cards for new zombies and bosses (Riot zombie D14 10, Stalker D16 21, Butcher D19 21) gave useful "What to do" lines. After that, the game's teaching got in the way:
- The radio repeated "rain Barrel has no Farmer: change someone's profession" or "garden Plot has no Farmer" on D12 08, D13 08, D14 08, D15 08, D16 08, D17 08 and D19 08, even though I had 2–3 Farmers each time (they were "hiding" all day from walkers). I followed it three times (Rosa D12 09, Leo D14 08, Maya D19 08), which made my defence weaker. Defense was 0/20 in the week 2 review (D15 00).
- Key systems showed up only after they had hurt me: scrap upkeep (D10 18), the threat ceiling (D4 09, after three level-ups), legacy goals and the convoy (D9 00), "too few left to fight, they hide" (D13 09), and the comeback convoy (D18 08, after 15 deaths).
- Happiness: 1/20 (D8 review) and 0/20 (D15 review). Nothing in the game ever told me what happiness is or how to raise it.

## The one change you would make
Make rescue and bandaging dependable and readable. When someone is down, tell me in one line why nobody is reaching them (for example "blocked: 8 walkers between Amy and Ben") and what would fix it. Make `bandage` work whenever the shown cloth or scrap is at least 1. Almost all of my 15 deaths were people who lay down for 10 to 36 game hours while I watched; the game gave me no lever I understood.
