Every claim cites a day and a number from my notes (`runs/pilot1/builder_notes.md`) or the run log (`runs/pilot1/builder.jsonl`).
Played 3 full sessions of 140 game hours plus 28 h of a fourth (D1 08:00 → D20 00:00), with `away 22` at D7 04:00, D13 00:00 and D18 20:00. Stopped because day 20 started; server was still answering.

Final town, D20 00:00: Haven level 5, 4 people (peak 12 on D9–D10), beds 4/16, 31 buildings, land tier 2, food 100.1, water 49.9, scrap 26, stone 26, wood 1.

## Fun score: 3 / 10
Days 1–4 were a good builder's rush (three levels, first expansion); from D4 to D20 I gained one Haven level, was out of wood most of the time, could not get more land, and watched 10 of 17 residents die while I had no order to give.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D2 14:00–D4 08:00 — levels came as fast as I could build: L2 ready D2 14:00 (6/6 people, 6/6 buildings), L3 ready D3 08:00, L3 done D4 06:00, and at D4 08:00 I could level up again *and* expand (renown 70, land 0→1) in the same minute. That is the loop this profile wants.
2. D7 20:00 — Lodge (HQ2) finished: canBuild went from 12 to 24 types and canUpgrade from 0 to 7 lines in one step. The only moment in the run when I had a real plan to draw up.
3. D8 00:00 — "HAVEN REVIEW · WEEK 1 · GRADE B … Buildings 20/20, Defense 1/20, Happiness 2/20, total 61/120". Honest, specific feedback that I had over-built and under-defended. (Smaller highs: D11 12:00–17:00 one medium lumber trip took wood 9→45 and paid for the second expansion; D12 00:00 "Amy and Noah … are now a couple".)

## Three moments that lowered it
1. D1 08:00–D3 evening — the very first building I ordered, a 4-wood Garden Plot, stayed "farm (site)" for about 60 game hours while tent, workbench, canteen, crates and scrapyard, all ordered later, finished. Wood was 9–40 the whole time. It held one of only two build slots, and food fell to 3.9 (D3 01:00, "0.6 days left") and 2.7 (D4 00:00) because of it. Nothing ever said why.
2. D7 08:00–D13 00:00 — the town was beaten flat and I could only watch. Raiders stayed inside for 14 hours on D7 (08:00–22:00); D10 06:00 morning card: "Buildings damaged: 12 (361 damage)", all 10 residents listed hurt, 6 of 10 at "0% down"; six Farewell cards between D7 15:00 and D13 00:00 (Noah, Lena, Eli, Theo, Noah, Maya). In the last 18 hours of session 2 (D12 06:00–D13 00:00, three deaths) I gave zero orders because wood was 1–2 and every build and upgrade read "cannot afford".
3. D17 17:00 — `build lounge` → "no free land: expand"; expand → "needs a bigger HQ (land tier 3 needs HQ tier 3; HQ is tier 2)"; canUpgrade has no HQ tier 3. I had 37 wood, 39 scrap, 13 stone and nowhere to put anything. Land was stuck at 2 from D11 21:00 to the end, and canBuild kept listing all 24 types as affordable with no land warning.

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
It got in the way after day 4. A builder needs three things: materials, a place to put buildings, and a reason to pick one building over another. Wood came almost only from expeditions, and low-risk trips put healthy people on the ground (D5 10:00: June 60/60 and Noah both "collapsed" on a Low-risk Abandoned Houses run for 2 food and 2 water), so building speed was set by how fast scavengers healed, not by my planning. Land ran out with no visible path forward. And the game never told me what a building does for the town: beds read 4/8 with only the HQ, 9/10 with four tents, 10/14 with five; I could not tell what a tent adds. What it did serve: the first three days, the Lodge unlock, and the week review.

The cozy promise did not hold for this play style. By the morning cards, 5 of 9 residents were hurt on D5, 10 of 10 on D10, and the town ended with 4 people in 16 beds.

## The one change you would make
Always show the builder what is blocking the next step and give a way to act on it. Concretely: when a build, upgrade or expansion cannot proceed, state the cause on the button ("no free land — next land needs HQ tier 3, which unlocks at Haven level N", "site waiting: nobody free to haul", "12 fence tiles to go"), and let spare scrap buy wood at the Trading Post. In this run wood plus an invisible land cap decided everything from D4 to D20, and I was never told either was the problem until I ran into it.
