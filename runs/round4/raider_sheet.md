## Fun score: 6 / 10
The combat content I could reach was good: one stronghold, one faction raid and five boss nights. There wasn't much of it, though. Between fights, most of my time went on fighting broken pathing and supply stalls, not on getting stronger.

## Three moments that raised it
1. **D11 08–11, Toll Kings fort raid.** The raid shows a list of the 29 camp buildings, and you `sh hit armory` / `sh loot` in slices. The boss died by 45 s. Lena, Theo and Rosa all went down, and each `sh med` (4 used) brought one back. When enemies reached 0 at 140 s I could pick what to loot: armory, warehouse and storage filled the bag to 10/23/39/40. Haul: +25 renown, +9 scrap, +19 wood, +9 stone, a Road Sign (atk 32), a Bike Helmet and a Victory Banner. Best part of the run. It felt like a real raid with real choices.
2. **D9 09–10, Overrun Clinic stronghold.** Squad power was 208 against a recommended 175. Theo was at 17%, and `sh med` took him to 67%. The boss woke at 50 s, I called `sh focus`, and it died at 64 s (22 kills). Reward: Dr. Ines rescued, +9 meds, a Stronghold Banner, then Kevlar Vest and Medic Pouch. The call felt like it mattered.
3. **D13 08–13, Military Checkpoint (high risk).** +66 scrap, +24 meds, +32 fuel, "+122 ammo" and 3 items in one trip. That's the loot scale a raider wants. It came at a price (see Low 1). Boss nights were fun too: Gatling Boss killed at D6 23 with Military Armor dropped, Mutant at D10 00, Bloater Queen at D12 22.

## The one change you would make
Make the base's paths visible and fixable: when the HQ door or a storage door is blocked, point at the tile (or open a gate there automatically). In this run that one fault seems to explain the water walk-out (D8 00), the 9–14 h rescue loops, the slow Fort build and the wood stuck at 0. That kept a raider at home fixing logistics instead of raiding.
