# Evaluation sheet — Collector · build 3D.171 · seed 21 · days 1–20

I played for the Collector: get every field note, trophy, medal and item. I played 140 game h, then went away for 22 h, and repeated. My sessions were D1 08 to D6 20, D6 20 to D12 16 and D12 16 to D18 12, then D18 13 to D20 00. Sources are collector_notes.md and collector.jsonl.

## Fun score: 6 / 10
The game gives a collector a lot to chase: 16 field notes, 23 craftable items, 3 legacy goals, 2 banners, 3 dogs and weekly grades. Two things dragged the score down. A 68-hour farm site starved the first four days, and water ran out every day from D15 to D18 even though the daily line showed a surplus.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D9 07–08: the Overrun Clinic stronghold. My squad had power 238 against 175 recommended. The fight lasted 76 s: I called `sh med` twice and `sh focus` with the boss at 10%. It killed the Butcher of Ward 3 and gave +25 renown and +6 meds. It also rescued Callum (Medic) and raised a "Stronghold Banner" at D9 08. This was the best few minutes of the run.
2. D14 07–11: the Gadgets research unlocked 14 accessory recipes, so 23 craftables in total. I crafted 6 new items in one go: Lifting Gloves, Reading Glasses, Tool Belt, Wristwatch, Knee Pads and Walkie-Talkie, for 36 scrap. At D15 00 the week-2 review came: "GRADE S · 100/120 · Beacon of Hope" with a Tool Belt as the prize. For a collector, crafting a list down to nothing is the core pleasure.
3. Pets and allies paid off in ways I could see:
   - D7 00 stray dog Rusty went to Marcus, and D10 00 Scout went to Lena.
   - D12 09 "Scout is barking — raiders spotted near Lena!"
   - D7 08 I sent 2 meds to Greenwater. On D9 08 they thanked me with +12 food and +2 cloth.
   - D13 08 Greenwater offered an alliance, which gave the Alliance Banner.

## Three moments that lowered it
1. D1 08 to D5 04: the farm site stayed "farm (site)" for about 68 game h with wood on hand (37 wood at D4 18). Other sites finished in hours: the tent placed D4 11 was built by D4 12, and the crates went up in about 3 h. Food fell to 0.4 at D4 01 (−10/day), and only a free "comeback convoy" at D4 09 (+15 food, +15 water) saved the town. When the farm finally finished, food went from −10/day to −1/day (D5 06).
2. Water, D15–D18. The daily summaries said "Water +20.7 −15.0" (D16 00), "+22.6 −10.4" (D17 00) and "+25.6 −12.7" (D18 00). Yet every one of those days also said "Out of water: rations were short today". People were "dying of thirst" (Ben D17 00, Theo D17 01, Callum D17 09), and two people left because of it: Dante at D16 00 and June at D18 00. Stored water sat at 4–7. The same days kept showing "X can't reach a storage door with their load" (D8 14, D9 21, D10 07, D12 08, D13 08, D15 19, D17 09). I could not find or fix the cause with the tool, and I lost two collectible people to a number that said I was fine.
3. Low-risk trips collapsed my best people over and over:
   - D4 16 Marcus on Abandoned Houses.
   - D5 09 Lena (78/78 at the start) on the Lumber Yard.
   - D7 07 Lena and Marcus together, both at 100%, in stages 1–2.
   - D10 10 Noah (82/82 at the start).

   "Low" did not feel low. Every lumber run was a gamble, and it was the only reliable wood source.

So I used `rushlevel all` four times: D9 09, D10 08, D11 10 and D15 06, spending 125–225 renown each time. Without rushing I would have spent days waiting.
- The Fort site waited from D14 14 to D19 07, about 4.7 days, because every bit of wood went into it or into repairs (wood 0 at D16 13, D18 07). It finished at D19 07.
- Each 22 h away credited only 8 game hours of shifts (D6 20, D12 16, D18 13). That is fine, but it should be said up front.
