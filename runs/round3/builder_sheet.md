# Evaluation sheet — Builder · build 3D.171 · seed 21 · days 1–20

How I played: 3 long sessions, each about 140 game hours, with `away 22` between them (D1 08 to D7 07, D7 07 to D13 12, D13 12 to D19 08), then 16 game hours to the start of D20. I used renown to rush levels four times (L4 on D4 06, L5 on D6 07, L7 on D12 08, L9 on D14 08, L10 on D18 08). Notes: `builder_notes.md`. Log: `builder.jsonl`.

## Fun score: 6 / 10
The level track, the banners and the town filling up with couples and children kept me playing. Wood was short for 19 days straight, and every boss night knocked out half the town. For most of the run I was hauling wood and carrying the wounded, not building.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D18 14–15: I cleared the Overrun Clinic stronghold. Squad power was 246 against a recommended 175. I ordered `sh focus` and the boss died in 69 s. The haul was +25 renown, +9 scrap and +9 meds, a rescued medic (Dr. Ines) joined, and the Stronghold Banner opened level 11. This was the best moment of the run: a short, readable fight that paid off at once.
2. D7 10 to D7 22: I ordered the Trading Post (14 wood, 5 scrap, 2 cloth) and it raised the Civic Banner (D8 05), which opened level 6. A civic building as the way to the next era is exactly the goal a builder wants.
3. D9 00 and D14 00: babies were born (Ivy, then Finn and Nora), and on D14 00 Ivy grew up and joined as a worker ("Legacy: Next generation"). On D14 15 a lost-child errand brought Kai home, and he became a Guard on D17. The town grew from inside, which is very satisfying for this profile. Population went 4 (D1) → 12 (D8) → 19 (D20).

## Three moments that lowered it
1. Wood starvation, all game. Wood came from lumber trips (+15 on D2, +16 D3, +16 D3, +17 D4, +18 D5, +11 D7, +16 D8, +11 D11, +13 D12, +10 D16, +12 D17) or from idle people. Once everyone had a job, chopping stopped (D4 09–17: wood stuck at 18). Two staffed Lumber Camps still lost wood: 9 → 6 between D12 11 and D12 19, because repairs ate it. On D18 08 I had scrap 1 and wood 0. HQ3 unlocked on D12 08, but I could not order it until D19 20.
2. Boss nights wrecked the town about every 3 days: D6 21, D9 21, D12 21, D15 21, D18 21. On D9–D10, 11 of 13 people were down or in hospital (D10 04 list), and walkers stayed in town all of D10. Bosses "slunk back… It will return" at dawn (D13 06, D16 06, D19 06), so I couldn't even beat them. The morning after the D10 night showed "Hurt: Amy, Maya, Ben, Eli, June, Rosa, Hattie, Theo, Hana, Marcus, Mina". Nobody died, but nights felt like damage control, not building.
3. Building got held up in ways the game never explained. Only two builds can run at once ("two builds already running (the limit)", D1 12, D1 18, D2 11, D5 14). A Medical Tent ordered on D10 15 stayed a site until D16 00 because I had 0 cloth, and it held one of the two build slots for about 5 days with no warning. On D3 11 a barrel had no room ("no spot in the build area will take it… 'A wall or fence is in the way' on 12 spots") right after I expanded the land on D2 20.

- Level timers: L4 25 h, L5 43 h, L6 3 days, L7 84 h, L8 4 days, L9 108 h, L10 120 h, L11 144 h. Renown made them bearable. L8 (D13 12) and L11 (D19 08) both finished while I was away for 22 real hours, which feels good.
- Waiting for wood. With a full 2-slot build queue (or a slot frozen by a stalled site), I spent whole stretches just playing hours: D4 09–17, D6 10–18, D12 11–19, and D19 08–20 (waiting 12 game hours to scrape 20 wood together for HQ3).
- Quarry stone rate was about 2 per 11 h (D14 09 to D14 20). A single Old Quarry trip gave 17 stone (D15 09), far better than the building.

## Over too quickly or too easy
- Population and happiness goals came too easily. The week-1 review on D8 00 scored "Population: 20/20" with 12 people, and the week-2 review was grade A 94/120 while the town was getting flattened every few nights. Most people show ♥100 at D20 00.
- Rushing levels was cheap: L10 cost 100 renown on D18 08 and I still had 127 left. Renown was never the constraint. Wood was.

## Stuck
- HQ3 / land tier 3 (D12 08 to D19 20). Needs 20 wood, 30 stone, 12 scrap, 2 fuel. I tried lumber trips, a 2nd Lumber Camp (D8 08), a Quarry (D14 09) and an Old Quarry trip (D15). I had the stone by D16 15, but wood and scrap kept being spent on repairs. On D17 18: "Upkeep at midnight is 10.4 scrap and you have 8". I got out on D19 20, just before the end.
- The night spiral on D9–D10 and again D12. I tried policy defend, `radiohelp` (D9 23, worked: "cleared 3 zombies… carried 4"), `rescue NAME` and `bandage`. `militia` always said "healthy civilians band together when no fighter is left". I got out with nobody dead, but only by playing through it.

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
Both. The long goals serve a builder well: levels, banners, HQ tiers, land, cabins and fields. The level 6 and level 11 banners were the best design in the run. But the economy got in the way. Wood has one weak building (Lumber Camp, about 1 wood per hour when staffed, and its workers get downed outside the walls almost daily: Noah D7 12, Mina D8 18, D12 11, D18 11). Repairs and upkeep then eat that wood. Also, rushing levels raises the threat ceiling, so the "bigger, better town" goal fights the defence I didn't build. That one is partly my own fault: I built no traps and no guard posts besides one `post` (D11 12), and crafted only two weapons (D16 08).

## The one change you would make
Make wood flow keep up with a growing town. Either let Lumber Camps produce well (and upgrade) without sending their workers into the trees every day, or stop repairs and building upkeep from eating the same small wood pool. And flag a build site that is stuck on a missing material (my Medical Tent waited on cloth for 5 days).
