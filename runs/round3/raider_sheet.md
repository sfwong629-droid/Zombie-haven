# Evaluation sheet — Raider · build 3D.171 · seed 21 · days 1–20

Play habit followed: 72 game h, then `away 20`, six sessions (away at D4 8h, D7 8h, D10 8h, D13 8h, D16 8h, D19 8h). Stopped at D20 1h. The run ended with 17 adults in town (plus babies Nora and Kai still growing) and nobody dead.

## Fun score: 6 / 10
The two camp fights at the end (D18 Toll Kings' fort, D19 Blackgate Prison) are exactly what a Raider wants. But they came after 17 days with one stronghold, no faction to fight, and a town where a fighter collapses most nights.

## Three moments that raised it
1. D18 12–15h: the raid on the Toll Kings' fort. I picked targets with `sh hit armory / warehouse / storage / hq3`, healed Marcus from 43% to 93% with `sh med`, and the boss was dead at 45 s with 16 kills. Bag 40, fight lasted 244 s. Rewards: Spiked Club (atk 36, my first weapon better than a katana), Road Sign, a Katana, stone 3→13, +23 renown, Victory Banner, and "leader down: they scatter for 60 days". Picking which building to knock down is the best idea in the game for this profile.
2. D19 15–16h: Blackgate Prison at power 332 against a recommended 350. In one 15 s slice Jonah (64%) and Lena (71%) dropped to 0, with the Warden at 9%. I called `sh focus` and he died. The fight lasted 77 s, and Grant (a Guard) was rescued and came home along with an Armored Van. It was tense and I won by the call I made.
3. D4 20h: my first crafted Katana (21 scrap) took Marcus from power 33 to 64. On D6 22h the Gatling Boss died and dropped a second Katana for Jonah, which put the top four at 175, exactly the Clinic's recommended power (D7 0h). Gear-driven progress felt great early on.

## Three moments that lowered it
1. D16 9h to D19 6h: daytime raiders plus walkers caused chains of collapses. On D16: Amy 9h, Rosa 10h, Noah 11h, Esme 11h, June 13h, Eli 13h, Lena 14h, Maya 21h. At D19 4h six people were down at once (Amy, Esme, Cyrus, Finn, Marcus, Jonah). The news is a wall of "X is rescuing Y" lines. Lena went down at D16 14h and was only picked up at D17 2h, 12 game hours later, even though I ordered `rescue Lena` at 19h. Marcus was down from D15 22h until he reached the Medical Tent at D16 6h. As a Raider I spent these days nursing people instead of raiding.
2. D11–D16: the Gadgets research was refused five times with a reason that contradicts itself: "needs 30 research points (have 31) and 8 scrap (have 25)" on D11 7h, "(have 40) … (have 57)" on D13 16h, "(have 31) … (have 61)" on D16 8h. Medicine and Firearms were accepted at the same moments. So one quarter of the gear tree was locked for me.
3. D8 6h: the Clinic stronghold paid +25 renown, +9 scrap and +6 meds, but no gear. "Only 4 fit through: Maya stayed home" only appeared after I had sent five. The `power` tool had happily counted all five (205 for five on D7 0h). The prison paid the same way on D19 (+25 renown, +12 scrap). Boss kills in strongholds gave no weapons, while night bosses in town did (Katana D6, Crossbow D10, Kevlar D13).

## Waiting with nothing to do
- D8 7h to D18 8h, about 10 game days and 3 sessions: the Clinic was the only stronghold or raid I could attempt. Prison unlocked at L9 on D11 7h with 350 recommended and my power at 256. Every raid said "LOCKED: Meet X first", with no hint of how to meet a faction. The Foundry finally visited on D12 8h (raid recommended 500) and the Toll Kings on D18 8h (300).
- The power grind stalled: top four were 229 on D8, 256 on D10, 288 on D13, 306 on D16, 322 on D18. Gear stopped helping after D4. A katana is atk 32, a fire axe is also atk 32, and a shotgun is atk 34: Lena went 80→80→82 with katana, then kevlar, then shotgun (D11, D13). Armor never adds power: biker, firecoat and army helmet for three fighters left the squad at 229→229 (D8 6h). From then on, power only came from people levelling.
- Each `away 20` froze healing. I logged off at D7 8h with Marcus at 19% and Lena at 33% and came back to the same numbers, which cost the Clinic attempt one more day.

## Over too quickly or too easy
- Both strongholds were short: Clinic 59 s, Prison 77 s. The raid was 244 s, but most of that was looting.
- Rushing levels with renown was very cheap once I noticed it. On D10 8h I had 453 renown banked without noticing, and rushed L7 for 25 then L8 for 200 on the spot. On D11 7h I rushed L9 for 225, and on D17 2h L11. Level gates stopped mattering. That is not a problem for this profile, but nothing told me renown was piling up.
- Night bosses were easy until D15. The Gatling Boss died in 1 h (D6). The Bloater Queen died to two pipe bombs (D9–10). The second Bloater Queen on D15 was not killed ("slunk back").

## Stuck
- Factions: from D1 to D12 there was no way to meet any faction. I tried expeditions everywhere (houses, pharmacy, lumber, mall, warehouse). Factions come on their own schedule, and I did not get out of this by anything I did.
- Water: short almost the whole run (D2 1.7 days left, D5 1.8, D8 9.3 water, D12 0.9 days, D20 2.8 days). The Rain Barrel needs a Farmer worker, which I only found out on D1 17h from `workplaces`. I got by with Abandoned Houses trips (+7 to +12 water each) and storms.
- Wood: often at 0–2 (D7 8h 0, D14 6h 1, D17 7h 1, D20 0). The Lumber Camp worker was outside and went down again and again (Rosa D7 and D8, Ben D11 18h, Finn D17 3h and 9h). My fix was Lumber Yard trips (medium gave +38 on D3 and +47 on D17).
- Wall gap "15,15: an open tile in an inner fence" could never be closed. `wall 15 15` and `wall 15 15 gate` return `ok:false` with no reason (D9 7h). The perimeter stayed "sealed": false after 65 tiles.

## Did the game serve your goal or get in its way?
Mostly in its way until D18, then it served the goal well. The gear loop worked from D3 to D6: Bladesmithing, then the Workshop, then the Katana. After that it went flat, because armor does not count and every weapon sits around atk 32–36. Faction contact is out of the player's hands, and you cannot raid what you have not met. The camp raid on D18, with `sh hit`, `sh loot`, choosing a building and a looting bag, is the best system for this profile, and I only got it on day 18 of 20. Medium and high expeditions also served the goal: the Mall on D10 gave +33 food, +27 scrap and +21 cloth, and the Warehouse on D11 gave +59 scrap. But Marcus (L4, katana, 100% HP) collapsed on a LOW Pharmacy trip on D5 12h, and Noah on a LOW Lumber trip on D4 18h, so "low risk" did not feel low.

## The one change you would make
Let the Raider start the faction loop: make one faction (for example the Toll Kings' toll demand) find the town by about day 5, or let a high-risk expedition "find" a faction camp. Then the `sh hit` / `sh loot` camp raid, the best system in the build, arrives in the first week instead of on day 18 of 20.
