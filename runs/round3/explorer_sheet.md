## Fun score: 6 / 10
For an Explorer the trip layer is the best part of the game (event cards, odd finds, factions that remember you), but 3 of my 4 best fighters died on MEDIUM trips with no way to see it coming, and the town at home spent days 10–19 in a loop of people going down that I could only half steer.

1. D12 07 — The Overrun Clinic stronghold (recommended 175, my squad of 4). Sana dropped to 7%, `sh med` brought her to 57%; Eli went DOWN at 60 s and `sh med` revived him to 50%; `sh focus` then killed the boss ("The Butcher of Ward 3"), 24 kills in 66 s. My calls changed the outcome. The best 2 minutes of the run.

Choices that come back later feel like a living world.
3. D18 07 — Military Checkpoint (high risk, 4 people, power 216): +45 scrap, +32 meds, +25 fuel, +129 ammo, Medic Pouch, Hazmat Suit, Wristwatch and a grenade, nobody died. Also small delights along the way: the treehouse on D1 09 that revealed a new lead, the sick stranger who gave a Lucky Charm (D7 13), the towed Pickup Truck (D13 17), Marcus eating the gas-station hot dog (D16 09), babies Ivy (D14 00), Finn (D18 00) and Nora (D19 00), Ivy growing up into my 15th person (D19 00).

Mostly served it. As an Explorer I visited every expedition destination that unlocked (Houses, Pharmacy, Lumber Yard, Old Quarry, Shopping Mall, Warehouse, Gas Station, Military Checkpoint), cleared one stronghold (Overrun Clinic), met three factions (Greenwater D3 08, Toll Kings D6 08, Foundry D10 08), and followed every radio/lead card I was offered (radio signal D4 08, supply drop D7 08).
