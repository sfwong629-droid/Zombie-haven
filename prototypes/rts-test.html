Tap a fighter to select · tap ground to move there · tap a zombie to attack it. Orders last 20 s, then the AI takes over again. When the boss shows a red circle, move people out.

What this tests
  • Tap-to-command: move and attack orders with a flag; fighters go back to their own AI after 20 seconds.
  • Rally flag: every free fighter gathers at one spot and holds.
  • Stances: Aggressive chases far, Defensive stays near its spot, Hold fire only fights back.
  • Slow time: the game runs at 0.3× while a fighter is selected, so you can give orders on a phone.
  • Abilities: one per profession, with a cooldown.
  • Boss phases: the boss warns before a big smash; move your people out of the circle.
