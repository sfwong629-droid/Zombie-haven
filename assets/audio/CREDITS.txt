Zombie Haven: music sample credits (3D.53)

Instrument samples (folders fid, gtr, nyl, bass, banjo, harp, vibes, tpt, str, mbox, whis, ep, cello, brass, timp, piano):
  FluidR3_GM sound font by Frank Wen, rendered to MP3 by the midi-js-soundfonts project (github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Re-encoded to mono and shortened.

Percussion samples (folder kit):
  Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), github.com/sgossner/VCSL. Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain).

The music itself is generated by the game (js/sound.js) and is original.

sfx/ - recorded effects (axe chop, wood and metal impacts, pizzicato jingle) from the Kenney RPG Audio, Impact Sounds and Music Jingles packs, CC0 (kenney.nl), via github.com/Mcamento8/open-game-sfx-index.
