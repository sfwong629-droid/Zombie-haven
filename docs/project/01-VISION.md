# Zombie Haven: vision (approved by the owner, 16 Nov 2026)

The owner went through the draft line by line on 16 Nov 2026 and approved everything here. Change it only on the owner's say, and log the change in `02-DECISIONS.md`.

Lines marked **not in the game yet** describe where the game should go; they are not built yet.

## The game in one line
A cozy zombie-survival town builder for the phone: you grow a small camp of named survivors into a decent-sized town, keep them alive and happy, check in on them every day, and watch them fight off hordes of zombies.

## The feeling (owner, 16 Nov)
Small town → build → gear up → a horde attacks → you win, and it feels good. Lots of small features keep you engaged. You slowly see the town get bigger: more buildings, new buildings unlocked, new gear unlocked.

## How it should feel
- **A quick, rewarding start, then a long game.** "Not a game to finish in a few sittings": after the first levels, a level takes effort and about a check-in a day (owner, 11 Nov).
- **The town starts small and grows to a decent size.** The pay-off is watching your survivors fight a horde of zombies (owner, 16 Nov).
- **Building comes first.** Raiding and strongholds are a side activity next to building (owner, 11 Nov), but some things can only be done or won by raiding or doing strongholds (owner, 16 Nov).
- **You care about people, not units.** Survivors have names, jobs, traits, moods, bonds, couples, babies and pets.
- **Zombies are a threat day and night.** Nights stay the biggest fights. By day, small groups roam outside the walls and threaten anyone working or travelling there, and every few days a daytime horde comes that the radio warns about hours ahead, so there is time to gear up and repair. No constant daytime attacks on the town. (owner, 16 Nov; **not in the game yet**: today the danger is mostly at night.)
- **Dangerous, but fair.** The game never kills without a warning or a choice: no hidden odds, low risk never kills, every trip card offers turn back.
- **Funny, not grim.** Facts first, joke last. The radio DJ is the voice that teaches the game.
- **Calm to play.** Menus pause the game. No forced slow-motion. Being away is never punished: it only adds production.
- **The player always knows why.** When something is stuck or refused, the game says what and how to fix it.

## Pillars
1. **A town that looks alive**: people walk, carry, build, talk; faction camps look like tidy little towns, not scattered props.
2. **Clear choices with visible odds**: costs, risks and reasons are shown before you commit.
3. **More control than Fallout Shelter when it matters**: in strongholds and raids you steer the squad; in town people look after themselves, and when the town is attacked a **Take command** button lets the player steer the fighters with the same controls as in raids. It is optional: left alone, the fighters fight on their own, civilians always look after themselves, and the town must still win a fair fight without the player (owner, 16 Nov; **not in the game yet**).
4. **Factions react to how you treat them**, not to how well you are doing ("more renown should not make you more hated").
5. **A long road**: eras gated by Banners, with more than one way to earn each (fight, ally, build, celebrate); then weekly crises, seasonal strongholds, legacy starts, the City. The routes must be balanced so the player doesn't always take the easy one to get it over with (owner, 16 Nov).

## The look
- Phone shape, always. 3D low-poly town, chunky and readable. No chibi.
- UI: frosted glass panels with full-colour sticker icons (see `04-UI.md`).
- Zombies never carry weapons. The one exception is the Gatling Boss, who keeps the gun on his arm as he is (owner, 16 Nov).
- Leaders are not drawn bigger than their people; they have bodyguards.
- Armour and gear: low-to-medium detail, clean shapes, flat colours.
- A little gore is fine (owner, 16 Nov).

## Hard rules
- Works fully offline.
- Characters are never driven by Claude or any AI service (it has to work offline).
- One news pill at a time (the small rounded news message on screen; see `04-UI.md`). Emergency slow-down stays off.

## What the game is not
- Not a pay-to-wait strategy game: timers exist, but renown and play shorten them.
- Not a horror game: a little gore is fine, but no despair spirals.
- Not an RTS: the player never has to micro-manage the town to keep people alive.
- Not an idle game: the player's decisions decide who lives.

## Where the game falls short of this (owner, 16 Nov)
- Zombies are only a real threat at night; they should be a threat at any time of day.
